Privacy Policy for Centwise

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Centwise does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or financial data. Every expense you log is stored only on your own device. Centwise never asks you to connect a bank account, card, or any financial institution — you enter amounts manually, and that's the only financial information the app ever touches.

What data we collect

None. Centwise has no network access permission and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The app cannot connect to your bank, card, or any financial account — there is no such feature in this app at all.

Where your data is stored

All expenses and categories are saved in a local database (SQLite) inside this app's private storage area on your device. We have no access to it.

Data sharing

Because no data ever leaves your device, none is shared with any third party.

Backups

If your device has system backup enabled, your expense data may be included in your device's standard operating-system backup, the same way your other app data would be. This is managed entirely by your device's operating system, not by Centwise directly.

Data deletion

You can delete any expense at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

Centwise does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date, before that change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to your-email@example.com.
